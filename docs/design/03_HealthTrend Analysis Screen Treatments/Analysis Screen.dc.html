Each reading updates two hidden states: the level and its velocity. A reading pulls the level toward itself in proportion to how much noise the model expects, so a single high morning moves the line very little and the velocity state even less.

The interval reported beside the estimate is the posterior spread of that level. It widens when readings are sparse or irregular and narrows when they are dense, independently of which direction the level is moving.

Parameters are fixed and documented rather than fitted to this history; the model is not presented as a universal winner. Read the Method for the derivation.
