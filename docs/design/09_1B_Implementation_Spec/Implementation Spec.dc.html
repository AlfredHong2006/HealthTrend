1B Editorial — implementation spec

The adopted visual language for the HealthTrend analysis surface. This document freezes it: tokens, the layout table at 1440 / 390 / 320, every screen and state, the Inspect analysis tier behaviour, and the list of values that must be supplied by the real model before this ships.

1 · What is frozen

The architecture does not change: a chart hero with the estimate and the rate above it, an editorial rail beside the chart, a tier‑2 statistics band below it, one Inspect analysis entry that opens Why / Evidence / Statistics inline, and Method as a separate nav destination. The rail is a rail — grouping comes from alignment, spacing and typography, never from cards, boxes or panels. There are no cards anywhere in this design.

Two rules govern every value on screen. First, nothing is displayed that the model does not produce; where the model produces nothing, the slot is absent with a plain statement of why, not a dash or a placeholder. Second, parameters are fixed and documented — there is no per‑user fitting and no EM step, and no copy may imply either.

3.1 The four reflow decisions

The rail moves below the statistics band and becomes a single full‑width column; its left hairline becomes a top hairline. It stays one stacked column of hairline‑separated blocks. It must not become cards on the way down.

Gutters come down to existing steps — 32px (--space-8) at 390, 24px (--space-7) at 320. No new spacing token.

The 40px chart escape is replaced, not kept or dropped. At narrow widths the chart full‑bleeds symmetrically to both screen edges (negative margin equal to the gutter, padding restored on the legend row beneath it). The chart is the only element permitted to touch the screen edge, which reads as deliberate rather than as a shrunken desktop chart.

The hero drops one existing tier and the rate follows it — 72 → 46px and 46 → 30px, both existing sizes, no new intermediate step. The two‑step gap and therefore the reading order survive: the rate is still the second thing read. The vertical hairline between hero and rate becomes horizontal, with the rate directly beneath the hero.

Tabular figures must not wrap or truncate: ledger value spans are white-space: nowrap and the interval takes the remaining width and wraps instead. The widest lb values — a three‑digit level, a level range and a signed rate — are the cases to test.

4 · TrajectoryChart density

The chart's furniture reservation is fixed in the component, keyed off the width the component already measures. There is no density prop and no caller coordination: a chart placed in any column gets the right furniture. One invariant — the y‑axis is never off screen, and no data is hidden at any density.

Three consequences to preserve. The terminal flag is boxed at full width, becomes an inline azure value above a leader line at compact (same information, no filled box, 76px cheaper), and is absent below 360 because the hero states the identical number two blocks above. Below 360 the chart is therefore correct only on a screen that carries the hero — true of every screen in the product, but a constraint to honour if the chart is ever reused alone.

Axis ticks drop to zero decimals below 640 and from five to three. A tick is a scale, not a claim; the hero, the crosshair readout and the ledger all keep full precision. This makes the system's “axis tick: one decimal” precision rule density‑dependent, and that should be written into the precision tokens when this lands.

Month ticks thin by measured spacing, not by a caller‑supplied step, and a label repeated by thinning is dropped — at narrow widths only, so the desktop axis is untouched. The goal label moves from the plot's right edge to its left edge below 640, clear of the projection.

Guard, and the reason for it: a zero measurement means not measured yet, never a width. The mount host can be display: contents, and a zero reaching the density selector silently renders the 320 design on a 1440 screen with a zero‑width viewBox. Zero‑width observer entries are ignored, measurement also runs on mount / next frame / at 120ms, and all geometry reads one width that is never zero, defaulting to the full density.

5 · Screens

5.1 Analysis

Hero: estimated level, one decimal, with its 68% half‑width at two decimals on the line beneath, plus the as‑of clause. Rate adjacent to the hero at the display tier with its own air — it never joins the statistics band, where it would read as one diagnostic among many. Then the chart, its legend row, and the tier‑2 statistics band: 7 / 30 / 90‑day forecasts, 90‑day change, readings used, and residual scatter. The rail carries the lede, the raw reading for today with its residual, the gap note, and the range control.

5.2 Method

A nav destination, not a modal and not a tier of Inspect. Long‑form Source Serif 4 prose at the prose measure, mono notation, margin notes, one figure well. It explains the level and velocity states, what the credible intervals mean, why parameters are fixed and documented rather than EM‑fitted, and why short‑history per‑user fitting is not justified by the evaluation. It makes no claim about model behaviour beyond what the analysis screen already relies on. Its figure is a fixture and labelled as one.

6 · Inspect analysis

One entry point below the chart opens a deeper region in the main column — never inside the rail. It reveals one tier at a time; opening one closes the others. Closing returns to the surface with the chart and the hero/rate band untouched. The three tiers, in order:

{{ t.name }} {{ t.body }}

Layout: the tier body sits in a prose column with a margin‑note column beside it at 1440 (34em + 300px, 88px gutter); at 390 and 320 the note column moves below the body and its left hairline becomes a top hairline. Evidence's metric row is four‑up, two‑up, then one‑up. The measurement table drops its trend column and goes dense at both narrow widths. The statistics ledger's column headers are suppressed below 1440 rather than misaligning, and each row becomes label / symbol over value / interval.

Inspect is unavailable, not empty, when there is no span: all three tiers describe an estimated trajectory and the model has produced none. The entry point states that in one sentence and does not open.

8 · Required at implementation

Every item below is a value this design displays a slot for, or deliberately omits, that only the real model or the real evaluation can supply. Until each is supplied, the slot must stay absent or stay labelled — not filled with an approximation.

8.1 Velocity posterior interval

The rate carries no interval in this design. The fixture series generates a deterministic velocity and carries no velocity variance, and an OLS slope standard error is not the uncertainty this model produces, so no substitute was used. At implementation the rate must take its 95% interval from the model's own posterior for the velocity state. Three places consume it:

{{ v.where }} {{ v.what }}

When the posterior lands: the rate prints two decimals per week, always signed, with its interval in the qualifier tier beside it. If that interval spans zero, TrendDelta must switch to direction="flat".

8.2 Evaluation figures

Method argues that parameters are fixed and documented rather than EM‑fitted, and that short‑history per‑user fitting is not justified by the evaluation. No evaluation figure is stated numerically anywhere in this design, deliberately: none exists to cite. The argument is currently carried by prose alone. At implementation, either supply the real evaluation figures — held‑out error for fixed versus per‑user‑fitted parameters, the history length at which fitting stops hurting, and the sample the comparison was run on — or leave the prose as prose. Do not add a number to make the argument look quantified.

8.3 Fixture values

Every number on both screens is generated by a deterministic fixture build for layout only. The generator, so it can be recognised and removed: level starts at 79.8 kg falling 0.27 kg/week with a small sinusoid; the 68% half‑width shrinks from 0.73 to 0.31 kg as readings accumulate, and 95% is 1.96× that; measurements are the level plus ±1.0 kg of seeded noise; one reading in seven is skipped after day 30; a reading more than 0.93 kg from the level is marked down‑weighted; the projection widens 0.028 kg/day for 90 days; the goal is 74 kg; the default window is 120 days. lb is a display conversion at 0.45359237.

Two quantities the design does not show, and should not gain: R² and p‑values. A state‑space filter does not produce them, and the statistics tier is values with their intervals, not a regression report.
